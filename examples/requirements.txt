Senior Data Scientist - Acme Cloud (Lisbon, Hybrid)

About the role
We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to join our growing ML platform
team. You will work end-to-end: from framing the problem with stakeholders,
through prototyping and evaluating models, to productionizing them at scale
on our Kubernetes-based data platform.

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain production ML systems handling terabytes of
  event data per day
- Partner with product, infra, and analytics teams to identify high-impact
  opportunities
- Mentor junior data scientists and review their work
- Contribute to our internal MLOps tooling (feature store, model registry,
  experiment tracking)

Required
- 5+ years of professional experience building ML systems in production
- Strong Python and SQL skills
- Hands-on experience with at least one of: PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Solid understanding of A/B testing and causal inference
- Excellent communication; comfortable working cross-functionally

Nice to have
- Experience with streaming systems (Kafka, Spark Streaming, Flink)
- Familiarity with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Pulumi)
- Knowledge of feature stores and modern MLOps platforms
- Background in time-series forecasting or NLP
- Experience scaling teams or projects

What we offer
- Competitive salary + equity
- Hybrid working from our Lisbon office
- Annual learning budget
- A small, opinionated team that ships

Keywords: scalable, production, ML platform, cross-functional, mentorship, MLOps,
real-time, feature store, experimentation, causal inference.
